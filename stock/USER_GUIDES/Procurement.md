# 📋 Procurement Team Guide

## Role Overview
As a **Procurement Officer**, you are responsible for maintaining stock levels, managing supplier relationships, and ensuring goods are ordered and shipped on time.

## Key Workflows

### 1. Creating a Purchase Order (PO)
1.  Go to **Purchases > New Purchase**.
2.  Select a **Supplier**.
3.  Add items:
    *   System will show current stock and reorder levels.
    *   Enter quantity and unit price.
4.  Click **Save as Draft** (for review) or **Create & Approve** (if you have permission).
5.  Once approved, click **Email to Supplier** (or download PDF).

### 2. Monitoring Shipments
1.  When a supplier confirms dispatch, go to **Shipments > New Shipment**.
2.  Select the **Purchase Order** to link items automatically.
3.  Enter tracking details:
    *   **ETD:** Estimated Time of Departure.
    *   **ETA:** Estimated Time of Arrival.
    *   **Tracking No:** Carrier's reference.
4.  Update status periodically (`In Transit`, `In Customs`, `Delivered`).

### 3. Calculating Landed Cost
Once shipment costs are known (invoice received from forwarder):
1.  Open the **Shipment**.
2.  Click the **Landed Cost** tab.
3.  Enter **Freight**, **Duty**, **Insurance**, and other fees.
4.  Check the summary and breakdown chart.
5.  Click **Calculate & Save**.
    *   *Tip:* Check "Update Product Prices" to auto-update the master catalogue with the new true cost.

## Best Practices
*   **Always** link Shipments to POs to close the loop.
*   Update **ETA** dates so the dashboard "Incoming" widget stays accurate.
*   Review **Supplier Performance** reports monthly to negotiate better terms.
