# 📦 Warehouse Team Guide

## Role Overview
As **Warehouse Staff**, your goal is accurate inventory. You receive incoming goods, ensure quality, and keep stock counts correct.

## Key Workflows

### 1. Receiving Goods into Stock
**Trigger:** A shipment arrives at the specific loading dock.
1.  Log in and check **Dashboard** for "Arriving Today" or "Pending Receipt".
2.  Go to **Shipments > In Transit / Delivered**.
3.  Open the correct Shipment ID.
4.  Click the green **Receive Goods** button.
5.  **Audit the delivery:**
    *   Count the physical boxes.
    *   Inspect for damage.
6.  Enter the **Received Qty** for each line item.
    *   *Note:* If damaged, enter the good quantity only and add notes.
7.  Click **Confirm Receipt & Update Stock**.
    *   This instantly increases stock levels and creates a new batch.

### 2. Stock Checks & Adjustments
If you find a discrepancy (e.g., lost or broken item):
1.  Go to **Inventory > Stock Adjustment**.
2.  Select the **Product**.
3.  Choose Type: **Adjustment (Minus)**.
4.  Enter Qty and Reason (e.g., "Damaged in warehouse").
5.  Save. This records the loss without affecting procurement stats.

## Safety & Compliance
*   **Batch Tracking:** Always check the Batch # on the box matches the system if multiple batches exist.
*   **Expiry:** Prioritize picking older batches (FIFO) unless instructed otherwise.
